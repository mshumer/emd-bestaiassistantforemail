2. SaneBox

Snack $4.54 a month, billed every 2 years

Filters your existing inbox into folders so only important mail stays in view; works with any email provider.

Best for: anyone who wants less clutter without changing email apps.

3. Gemini in Google Workspace

Business Starter $7 per user a month (list)

Google’s AI built into Gmail (and, on higher plans, Docs, Meet and more), included in Workspace business plans.

Best for: teams already on Gmail who want AI with no new app.

4. Fyxer

Free plan; Pro $37 per person a month yearly ($49 monthly)

An AI assistant inside Gmail or Outlook that sorts your inbox, drafts replies and takes meeting notes.

Best for: professionals who want their inbox triaged and replies drafted.

5. Jace

Plus $20 a month yearly ($25 monthly); free start

An AI email and calendar assistant that drafts replies and handles tasks, paid for in credits.

Best for: individuals who want one assistant across email and calendar.

6. Superhuman

Starter $25 per member a month, billed yearly

A fast email app for Gmail and Outlook with AI writing and triage.

Best for: people who live in email and want speed and keyboard shortcuts.

7. Shortwave

Business $30 per seat a month

An AI-first email client for Gmail with AI search, summaries and one-click replies.

Best for: Gmail users who want a faster email app with AI built in.
